The Core Problem

One Unverified Assumption
Underlies Everything

"That the truck receiving the load at the plant is the truck that was authorized for that load."

Today, truck sequencing at batch plants is managed manually by plant personnel. If a truck loads out of sequence — intentionally or unintentionally — there are no active digital verification methods at the plant to confirm a match. No stop gap prevents the wrong truck from receiving the wrong batch. Once loading begins, the batching process proceeds without independent confirmation of truck identity against the active load assignment.

Electronic ticketing systems assume correct physical execution rather than independently verifying it. Batch weights may be correctly generated but associated with the wrong truck. In some cases, where batch weights are not required, the process proceeds with no quantitative verification whatsoever — relying entirely on blind assumption.

Intellectual Property

Two Issued US Patents

Complete Load Solutions holds two active and enforceable United States patents covering the verification and automation layer that the industry is missing. Both patents are issued, both are enforceable, and both are active through 2037.

US Patent 10,417,610
Load Verification
Covers the system and method for verifying that the correct truck receives the correct authorized load at the discharge point before any material is discharged. The system reads a unique identifier affixed to the truck, compares it against the active load assignment in the dispatch database, and either authorizes discharge if the identifiers match or blocks discharge if they do not.

When a match is confirmed, the load is marked as certified and a tamper-resistant audit record is generated with timestamped evidence. When a mismatch is detected, the operator is presented with options — override with documented non-certified status, or re-scan. If at any point the discharge process is interrupted and resumed, the verification sequence resets before material flow continues.

This is the foundational verification layer that does not exist anywhere in the industry today.

Expiration 2037

US Patent 10,330,520
Plant Automation
Covers the system for automatically initializing the weighing and batching process when the correct truck is identified in the staging area — matching truck identity against the active load assignment before batching begins, without requiring manual operator intervention.

When a truck arrives at the staging area and its unique identifier is read and matched against the load queue, the system automatically authorizes and initiates the weight-up process for that specific load. If no match is found, the weigh-up is blocked and the operator is alerted.

This patent addresses the plant automation layer — removing manual sequencing errors, enabling fully automated and remotely operated plant environments, and completing the verification architecture from staging through discharge.
Status Active & Enforceable
Expiration 2037

Focus Staging Automation
